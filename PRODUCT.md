# Product

## Register

product

## Users

QA engineers, test leads, engineering managers, and workspace administrators who use the product during planning, execution, triage, and release-readiness reviews. They need dense operational screens that make project health, risk, ownership, and next actions easy to scan under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

QMaster is an in-house test management system for organizing projects, suites, cases, runs, automation signals, deployments, reports, and workspace access. Success means teams can understand quality status quickly, move from insight to action, and trust the product as the source of truth for QA workflow.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, and modern. The interface should feel professional and capable without becoming cold, generic, or over-decorated.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page hero layouts, nested card stacks, scattered dashboard widgets, generic purple-blue gradient gloss, low-contrast gray text, and decorative motion that slows down task flow. Avoid Qase-clone sameness when stronger hierarchy and clearer visual rhythm can make the product feel owned.

## Design Principles

- Clarity beats decoration: every layout, color, and motion choice should help users understand quality and risk faster.
- Density with rhythm: dashboards should stay information-rich while grouping related information tightly and separating workflows clearly.
- Semantic color first: color communicates state, priority, ownership, and action, not ornament.
- Familiar controls, sharper execution: use standard product patterns, but make spacing, alignment, typography, and feedback precise.
- Actionable insight: dashboards should lead users from status to the next useful action without hunting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve visible focus states, keyboard navigability, reduced-motion alternatives, touch targets of at least 44px where applicable, and color semantics that do not rely on color alone.
